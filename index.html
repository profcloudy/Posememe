Pose Meme Cam

Strike a pose. A meme lands on your face.

REC

Everything runs in your browser. Your video never leaves this laptop. First load takes a few seconds while the pose model downloads.

Tip: sit back so both shoulders and both hands are in frame, with light on your face. Turn on the 9:16 crop only when you're ready to record, since it cuts off the sides of the frame. Recording saves a .webm of the canvas (no audio). Drop it in CapCut to add sound effects and convert to mp4.

Poses

Upload your own anime meme per pose. Transparent PNGs look best, since the meme sits right on your face. Poses with no meme yet show a + and draw nothing on camera.
